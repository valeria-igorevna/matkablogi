    Introduction

    This Privacy Policy describes how Matkablogi collects and processes personal data of its visitors and website users. Privacy is respected and personal data is protected in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

    Gathering of Personal Information

    No personal data is collected from visitors who browse the website without a user account.
    The following data is collected from registered users (admin and content creators):
    Username and password (passwords are stored in encrypted form)
    Usage logs (e.g., adding and editing articles)
    In addition, the server or your browser may collect technical data such as:
    IP address
    Browser type and operating system

    Purpose of Data Collection

    The data is processed for website maintenance, content management, and user account administration. It also helps identify potential security incidents (analysis of log data).
